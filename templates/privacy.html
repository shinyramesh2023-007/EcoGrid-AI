Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information across the EcoGrid AI platform.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use EcoGrid AI, we may collect: account information (name, email, company, phone) provided at registration; plant and machine data collected through IoT sensors you connect to the platform; and usage data such as pages visited and dashboard interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to operate and improve the platform, generate energy and maintenance insights, respond to support and contact requests, and send account-related communications. We do not sell your plant data to third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text. Data in transit is encrypted, and access to plant-level data is restricted to authenticated accounts associated with that plant.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management (keeping you logged in) and a local preference for dark/light mode. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

5. Data Retention

Account and energy data are retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your energy usage and reports

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@ecogrid.ai or via the Contact page.
