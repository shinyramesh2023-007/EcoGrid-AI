About Us

Every factory leaks power. We help you find it.

Most MSMEs lose 15–30% of their electricity spend to idle machines, aging motors and undetected faults — because nobody can see energy use at the machine level, in real time.

Why We Exist

Industrial energy intelligence, built for MSMEs

EcoGrid AI combines low-cost IoT sensors, cloud analytics and predictive machine-learning models to give plant owners a live, machine-by-machine view of consumption — and tell them exactly what to fix.

Our Mission

Make industrial energy intelligence affordable for every MSME, not just large enterprise plants.

Our Vision

A future where every machine on the shop floor is monitored, predictable and sustainable.
